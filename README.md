# Awesome Wedding Planning [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> A curated list of software for planning a wedding — seating charts and floor plans, RSVP and guest management, registries, budgeting, websites, and the tools planners use professionally.

Wedding planning is a logistics problem wearing a nice dress: a guest list that changes weekly, a floor plan constrained by a venue you don't control, meal counts a caterer needs by a deadline, and a hundred small print deliverables at the end. This list covers the tools that handle those pieces, for couples doing it themselves and for planners doing it for a living.

## Seating Charts & Floor Plans
*Arranging people in a room, then getting it onto paper.*

* [PerfectTablePlan](https://www.perfecttableplan.com) - Long-standing desktop seating planner for Windows and Mac, with automatic assignment from proximity rules.
* [Prismm](https://www.prismm.com) - Formerly AllSeated; 3D venue diagramming and event visualization used across the venue and catering industry.
* [Social Tables](https://www.socialtables.com) - Cvent's event diagramming and seating product, aimed at hotels and venues.
* [Table Planner](https://www.tableplanner.com) - Browser-based table layout and guest assignment tool.
* [Wedding Charts](https://weddingcharts.com) - Feet-accurate floor plan canvas with eight table shapes, 27 venue objects, and CAD/DXF import at scale. Applies keep-together and keep-apart rules when arranging guests and flags violations rather than resolving them silently. Exports seating posters, tent and escort cards with cut guides, per-table caterer lists, and a QR guest-lookup page.

## All-in-One Planning Platforms
*Checklists, vendors, guest lists, and websites in one account.*

* [Hitched](https://www.hitched.co.uk) - UK-focused planning platform with venue and supplier directories.
* [Joy](https://withjoy.com) - Free wedding websites, RSVPs, registry, and guest management in a single product.
* [The Knot](https://www.theknot.com) - Vendor marketplace plus planning tools; one of the two largest US wedding platforms.
* [WeddingWire](https://www.weddingwire.com) - Vendor reviews and directory with planning tools, now under the same parent as The Knot.
* [Zola](https://www.zola.com) - Registry-led platform that expanded into websites, RSVPs, invitations, and planning checklists.

## Wedding Websites & RSVPs
*Collecting responses without a spreadsheet of text messages.*

* [Appy Couple](https://appycouple.com) - Wedding website and companion app with design-forward templates.
* [RSVPify](https://rsvpify.com) - RSVP and event registration with meal choices, plus-one control, and guest-list segmentation.
* [Say I Do](https://www.sayido.com) - Wedding website builder with RSVP tracking.
* [Squarespace](https://www.squarespace.com) - General site builder frequently used for custom wedding sites where templates feel limiting.

## Registries & Cash Funds
*Where the gifts actually go.*

* [Blueprint Registry](https://www.blueprintregistry.com) - Universal registry organized room by room.
* [Honeyfund](https://www.honeyfund.com) - Cash and honeymoon registry with low or no fees depending on payment method.
* [MyRegistry](https://www.myregistry.com) - Universal registry aggregating items from any retailer.
* [Zola Registry](https://www.zola.com/wedding-registry) - Universal registry with cash funds and group gifting.

## Budgeting & Guest Lists
*The two spreadsheets everyone starts with.*

* [Google Sheets](https://sheets.google.com) - Still the default for budget and guest tracking; templates are widely shared.
* [WeddingHappy](https://weddinghappy.com) - Task and timeline app that schedules planning milestones backward from the date.
* [Wedding Planner by Zola](https://www.zola.com/wedding-planning/checklist) - Free checklist and budget tracker.

## Stationery & Print
*Invitations, save-the-dates, and day-of paper.*

* [Basic Invite](https://www.basicinvite.com) - Invitation printing with unusually granular color customization.
* [Greenvelope](https://www.greenvelope.com) - Digital invitations with RSVP tracking, designed to look like printed stationery.
* [Minted](https://www.minted.com) - Independent-artist marketplace for invitations, save-the-dates, and day-of signage.
* [Paperless Post](https://www.paperlesspost.com) - Online invitations and cards with tracked delivery and RSVP.

## Tools for Professional Planners
*Client management for people who do this every weekend.*

* [Aisle Planner](https://www.aisleplanner.com) - Planning, design, and client collaboration suite for wedding professionals.
* [Dubsado](https://www.dubsado.com) - Client management, contracts, and invoicing used widely by event and creative businesses.
* [HoneyBook](https://www.honeybook.com) - Proposals, contracts, payments, and scheduling for client-based small businesses.
* [Planning Pod](https://www.planningpod.com) - Event management with diagramming, budgets, and vendor coordination.
